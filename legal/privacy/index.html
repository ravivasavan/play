Privacy

Last updated 11 Sep 2026 · Ezra (personal Google OAuth client)

This page covers the desktop OAuth client Ravi Vasavan uses so his assistant “Ezra” can read and manage mail and calendars he authorises on his own machine.

It is a single-user tool. It is not a public product and is not offered to other people.

  • Only Google accounts Ravi signs in are accessed (Gmail and/or Calendar scopes he grants).
  • Tokens are stored on his computer for that client. Access tokens rotate; refresh tokens are used only to keep that access working.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this page.
  • No selling of personal data. No third-party marketing use.
  • Google’s own privacy policy also applies to data held in his Google accounts.
